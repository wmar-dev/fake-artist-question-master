How it works
  1. One person creates a game, and everyone else scans the QR code.
  2. The site deals a category and a secret word. One player gets only the category.
  3. Share one sheet of paper. Draw one mark each, clockwise, for two laps.
  4. Vote out loud for the fake artist, then the host reveals the answer.
